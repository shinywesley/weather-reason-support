Who we are

Weather Reason is an iPhone weather app published by Wesley Jaynes. This policy covers the app and this support website. The app is in release preparation. Subscription processing described below applies when purchases are enabled; subscriptions are not currently on sale.

The app does not require a Weather Reason account for its current weather and local saved-place features. This is not a promise that no data leaves your device: weather, search, map and purchase services need information to respond to your requests.

Location and weather requests

Location permission is optional. When you choose the location action, iOS can provide a location to select a nearby forecast. The current implementation requests location while you use the app, with an approximate-location accuracy target. It does not continuously track you in the background. The coordinates actually supplied by iOS may still identify a specific area.

You can search for a place without granting location permission. Apple processes place-search text and location naming through its map and geocoding services.

The connected weather service receives the latitude and longitude you select, including a searched or saved place, when it supplies point forecasts, map samples or comparison data. Requests also expose technical connection information such as your IP address, request time and app user agent to the receiving service and its hosting provider. For supported US locations, the service requests official point weather from the National Weather Service using the selected coordinates.

These requests support weather delivery, caching, reliability and abuse prevention. Avoid using place labels to store sensitive personal information.

Device storage and weather delivery

On your device: saved and recent places, custom forecast conditions, the selected place, map position, units, appearance and other preferences are stored locally. Forecasts, map images and catalogs may be cached for reuse. A compact forecast can be shared locally with the app’s widgets. Widgets can request updated weather using the saved place’s coordinates when iOS permits a background refresh; they do not request your moving device location. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Weather delivery: the public app reads published provider weather and uses bounded temporary caches for weather responses. It does not offer a cloud account, upload custom conditions, or save personal weather captures on the server.

Local preferences remain until removed, reset or deleted with the app; caches may be cleared by iOS. The application server disables access logging of coordinate query strings. Hosting and provider systems may retain technical records under their own policies; no fixed retention period is promised for those third-party systems.

Deleting the app does not remove Apple or RevenueCat purchase records. Contact the developer to request review or deletion of data within our control.

Subscriptions and RevenueCat

When subscriptions are enabled, Apple processes purchases. Weather Reason uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, determine Pro access, support restoration and track subscription status.

RevenueCat can process an app-generated anonymous customer identifier, purchase and transaction information, products, subscription status, and technical app/device information. “Anonymous” means we do not ask for your name to create this identifier; it can still link purchase activity over time. Apple and RevenueCat may receive network information needed to provide their services.

The app does not receive your full payment-card details. The subscription integration does not need your precise location, place-search text or saved-place names, and those are not supplied as RevenueCat customer attributes. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

Services that help the app work

  • Apple: location permission, MapKit previews, search/geocoding, App Store transactions and system services. See Apple Maps and privacy.
  • OpenFreeMap: weather-map basemap requests can reveal your IP address and the map tiles being viewed. Tiles represent geographic areas. Map rendering uses MapLibre, with OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap attribution. See OpenFreeMap’s privacy policy.
  • Weather service and data providers: selected coordinates and technical requests support weather delivery. Provider names and source information are shown with the relevant weather product. Regional availability varies.
  • RevenueCat: purchase validation and subscription management when enabled.
  • GitHub: this website and the public support issue tracker.

The current app does not include an advertising network or request permission to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. We do not sell personal information or use weather locations for targeted advertising. Providers may process information on servers outside your country under their own applicable policies.

Your choices and privacy requests

  • Use place search instead of device location, or revoke location permission in iPhone Settings.
  • Remove local saved places and custom conditions, and avoid adding personal details to place names.
  • Delete the app to remove its local data, subject to device backups and separately managed widget/shared storage.
  • Manage or cancel Apple subscriptions through your Apple Account. Cancellation is separate from deleting app data.

You may ask about access, correction or deletion of personal data within our control, or raise another privacy concern, by contacting Wesley Jaynes through the support page. Because issues are public, begin with a general request for a private follow-up; do not post a receipt, purchase identifier, exact location or other personal information. Some transaction or operational records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, fraud prevention or service purposes.

The service is not directed specifically to children. If you believe a child has supplied personal data to the developer, use the same privacy contact process.

This support website

These pages use local styles and images, with no analytics script, advertising pixel, embedded map, external font or contact form. GitHub Pages still receives connection information when serving the site. If you open a GitHub issue, GitHub processes your account information and the content you submit; that content is publicly visible. See the GitHub privacy statement.

This policy may be updated as the app and its services change. The date at the top identifies the latest version. Material new data uses will be described before they are introduced where required.
