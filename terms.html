Billing, renewal and cancellation

When available, Pro subscriptions are purchased through Apple’s in-app purchase system and billed to your Apple Account after confirmation. A monthly plan renews monthly; a yearly plan is billed in full and renews yearly. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled through Apple before renewal. Review the date and conditions shown by Apple for your subscription.

You can manage or cancel a plan in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Weather Reason. Deleting the app does not cancel the plan. See Apple’s subscription help.

Restore purchases from the app using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Access depends on a verified active entitlement. Canceling renewal generally leaves access until the current paid period ends, subject to Apple’s terms and any refund or revocation.

Apple handles App Store refund requests. Visit Report a Problem to ask Apple about an eligible purchase. Refund eligibility and consumer rights depend on your circumstances and location.

Using weather information

Forecasts are estimates and may change. Observations can be delayed, incomplete or outside the available coverage. Always check the displayed source, issue or observation time, valid period and units. A blank radar area does not establish that conditions are clear.

Weather Reason is for weather information and planning. It does not replace official emergency instructions or a dedicated warning service. The current preview does not provide continuous background tracking or push alerts. Follow local authorities and official weather services for urgent decisions.

Map and weather data belong to their respective providers and remain subject to their licenses and attribution requirements. Feature and regional availability depend on the underlying data and connected services.
