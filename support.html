Everyday questions

Can I use the app without sharing my location?

Yes. Search for a place instead. Location is optional and is requested when you choose the location action. You can change permission at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Weather Reason.

Why is the radar empty or older than expected?

Check the source and timestamp on the map. Radar coverage varies, and a clear area does not always mean dry weather. Data may be delayed, unavailable, or outside the source’s coverage. Refresh the view or try another supported layer. Saved weather keeps its original time.

How are radar and forecast maps different?

Observed radar shows captured observations. Forecast layers show a model’s prediction for the displayed time. Rainfall rate, accumulated rainfall and the chance of precipitation mean different things; use the layer’s legend, units and time window.

Where are my places and conditions kept?

Saved and recent places, custom forecast conditions, preferences and cached forecasts stay on your device. The public app does not upload your conditions or save personal weather captures on the server. See the storage details.

Does weather tracking send background alerts?

Custom conditions refresh when you open tracking or pull down to refresh. The app shows supported official alerts while you use it. It does not provide continuous background monitoring or push delivery. A saved place or tracking view is not an emergency notification service.

Why does a widget show an earlier update?

Widgets use a compact copy of the last shared forecast and show its original update time. iOS controls background refresh timing. Open the app and refresh the selected place to request newer available weather.

Purchase help

Subscriptions are being prepared and are not currently on sale. The guidance below applies when App Store purchases become available.

Restore access

Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase, then choose Restore purchases from the subscription screen. A pending purchase must complete before Pro access becomes active. If a verified active subscription is still missing, contact support.

Manage or cancel a subscription

On iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, then choose Weather Reason. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple’s cancellation instructions explain the available options.

Ask about a charge or refund

Apple handles App Store billing and refund decisions. Use Apple’s Report a Problem service or read Apple’s refund guidance. Do not post receipts or payment details in a public issue.

Contact the developer

Weather Reason is published by Wesley Jaynes. Report a problem, ask a question or request private help by emailing aicalorietracker@gmail.com. You can also use the project’s GitHub support page for general public reports. A GitHub account is required to create an issue.

Tell us what happened.

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and the steps that led to the problem. For a weather-data issue, the layer name and displayed timestamp are helpful.

Open the support page ↗

GitHub issues are public. Leave out precise locations, receipts, identifiers and other private information. For a private support or privacy request, email aicalorietracker@gmail.com directly.
